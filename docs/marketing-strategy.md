# HealthOS — Marketing Strategy

**Company:** Zenera Labs
**Product:** HealthOS — AI-Powered Hospital Revenue & Operations SaaS
**Version:** 2.0
**Last Updated:** September 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary

HealthOS is a hospital revenue-cycle and operational intelligence platform that starts as an overlay on existing hospital systems and expands toward broader management. Seven tightly connected modules reinforce one patient → encounter → billing → claim → revenue loop.

**One SaaS. Seven modules. One revenue-cycle workflow.**

---

## 2. Market Positioning

### What We Are
- AI-powered hospital revenue-cycle SaaS
- Platform overlay — works alongside existing HIS/HMIS/EMR
- Revenue-focused intelligence, not a generic ERP

### What We Are Not
- Not a hospital ERP replacement
- Not a chatbot or generic AI assistant
- Not a feature-count product

### Positioning Statement
> **For hospital CFOs and billing managers** who lose revenue to missed charges, denied claims, and manual chasing between departments, **HealthOS** is an **AI-powered revenue-cycle intelligence platform** that **finds lost money, prevents claim problems, and eliminates department-chasing** — unlike generic hospital ERPs that try to do everything and nothing well.

## 8. Brand Guidelines

### Voice & Tone
- **Confident** — we know hospital revenue
- **Clear** — no jargon, no fluff
- **Direct** — say what we mean
- **Empathetic** — we understand the pain

### Language Rules
- Use "find" not "discover"
- Use "stop" not "prevent"
- Use "money" not "revenue" (in patient-facing content)
- Use "AI" carefully — always pair with "explainable" or "auditable"
- Never claim "HIPAA compliant" — say "built for healthcare compliance requirements"

### Visual Identity
- **Primary:** Deep navy (#1a1a2e) — trust, stability
- **Accent:** Electric blue (#4361ee) — intelligence, technology
- **Success:** Emerald (#10b981) — positive outcomes
- **Warning:** Amber (#f59e0b) — at-risk items
- **Danger:** Rose (#ef4444) — critical issues
