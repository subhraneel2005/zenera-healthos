# HealthOS — Sales Playbook

**Company:** Zenera Labs
**Product:** HealthOS — AI-Powered Hospital Revenue & Operations SaaS
**Version:** 2.0

## 1. Sales Overview

### Sales Model
- **Product-Led Growth (PLG):** Free trial → paid conversion
- **Sales-Assisted:** Demo → pilot → enterprise contract
- **Target Contract Size:** ₹5L–₹50L/year ($6K–$60K/year)
- **Sales Cycle:** 30-90 days (pilot), 90-180 days (enterprise)

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Type** | Private hospital with insurance/TPA activity |
| **Size** | 50-500 beds |
| **Revenue** | ₹20Cr–₹500Cr annual |
| **Pain** | High denial rates, manual billing, no revenue visibility |
| **Tech** | Has existing HIS/HMIS, willing to integrate |
| **Decision Maker** | CFO, Finance Director, or COO |

## 2. Lead Qualification (BANT)

| Criteria | Qualifying Questions |
|----------|---------------------|
| **Budget** | "What's your current annual spend on RCM/billing software?" "Do you have budget allocated for revenue-cycle improvements?" |
| **Authority** | "Who makes the final decision on hospital software purchases?" "Is the CFO involved in this evaluation?" |
| **Need** | "What's your current claim denial rate?" "How long does reconciliation take?" "How do you track revenue at risk?" |
| **Timeline** | "When do you want to see results?" "Is there a fiscal year deadline?" |

### Qualification Scoring

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **A (Hot)** | Has budget, decision-maker involved, high denial rate (>15%), wants results in 30 days |
| **B (Warm)** | Has budget or authority, moderate pain, 60-90 day timeline |
| **C (Nurture)** | No budget yet, low urgency, needs education |

## 3. Discovery Questions

### Pain Discovery
1. "What's your current claim denial rate, and how does that compare to industry average?"
2. "How many hours per week does your billing team spend chasing departments for documents?"
3. "When was the last time you identified a missed charge after the patient left?"
4. "How do you currently track revenue that's been billed but not collected?"
5. "What happens when a claim gets denied? Walk me through the process."

### Impact Discovery
6. "What does that denial rate cost you annually?"
7. "How many billing FTEs could be redeployed if you automated claim validation?"
8. "What's the financial impact of not catching missed charges?"
9. "How much time does your finance team spend on reconciliation?"

### Solution Discovery
10. "If you could see every dollar at risk in real-time, what would you do differently?"
11. "What would it mean to submit claims that get paid the first time?"
12. "How valuable would it be to know the risk score of every claim before submission?"

## 4. Objection Handling

### "We already have a billing system."
> "HealthOS isn't a replacement — it's an intelligence layer on top of what you have. Your billing system handles transactions. We find the money it misses."

### "We can't afford another software."
> "What's the cost of your current denial rate? If we reduce denials by 30%, the platform pays for itself in 3 months. Let me show you the ROI calculator."

### "AI in healthcare is risky."
> "We agree — that's why every AI finding has evidence, a confidence score, and a reviewer trail. Our AI never silently edits bills. It flags issues for humans to review."

### "We need to think about it."
> "Totally understand. Can I send you the ROI analysis with your numbers? That way you have data to make the decision."

### "Our team is already overwhelmed."
> "That's exactly why this matters. HealthOS reduces the manual work — automated claim validation, reconciliation, and workflow management. Your team gets time back."

### "We've been burned by software before."
> "We start with a pilot — 30 days, one branch, synthetic data. You see real results before committing. No long-term contract for the pilot."

## 5. Demo Script (15 Minutes)

### Opening (1 min)
> "I'm going to show you how HealthOS finds lost revenue and prevents claim problems — in 15 minutes. Stop me anytime with questions."

### Dashboard (2 min)
> "This is the Revenue Leakage Radar. Every number here is real money — what's been billed, what's at risk, what's stuck. Your CFO sees this every morning."

**Show:** Dashboard with KPIs, revenue at risk, aging, denial trends.

### Bill Audit (3 min)
> "Here's an encounter. I click 'Run Bill Audit.' In 10 seconds, HealthOS checks every charge against payer rules and flags issues."

**Show:** Missed charge detected, duplicate charge flagged, package inconsistency found.

### Claim Risk (3 min)
> "Before this claim goes out, HealthOS scores its risk. This one is HIGH risk — missing documentation. Here are the reasons and what to do about it."

**Show:** Risk score, top factors, recommended actions, evidence links.

### Denial Intelligence (3 min)
> "This claim was denied. Instead of guessing why, HealthOS classified it, found the root cause, and generated an appeal draft with policy citations."

**Show:** Denial classification, root cause analysis, appeal letter draft.

### Reconciliation (2 min)
> "And here's where the money lands. HealthOS matches payments to claims automatically. When there's a discrepancy, it flags it with the dollar amount."

**Show:** Payment matching, variance detection, aging report.

### Close (1 min)
> "That's HealthOS. Seven modules, one platform, one revenue-cycle workflow. The question is — how much revenue are you currently losing that HealthOS would find?"

## 8. Pipeline Management

### Pipeline Stages

| Stage | Definition | Entry Criteria | Exit Criteria |
|-------|-----------|---------------|---------------|
| **Lead** | Inbound or outbound contact | Contact info captured | Discovery call scheduled |
| **Qualified** | BANT confirmed | Discovery call completed | Demo scheduled |
| **Demo** | Product demonstration | Demo completed | Pilot proposal sent |
| **Pilot** | 30-day pilot running | Pilot agreement signed | Pilot results reviewed |
| **Proposal** | Contract sent | Proposal delivered | Negotiation started |
| **Closed Won** | Contract signed | Payment received | Onboarding started |
| **Closed Lost** | Decision not to proceed | Loss reason documented | Nurture sequence triggered |

### Loss Reasons (Track These)
1. No budget
2. No authority (can't get CFO buy-in)
3. Timing (fiscal year, other priorities)
4. Competitor chosen
5. Build in-house decision
6. Not a fit (too small, wrong specialty)
7. Price objection
8. Technical concerns (integration, security)
